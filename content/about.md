## Why it exists

I've been reading city council agendas and minutes for a few years now, mostly because
I wanted to understand how a decision gets from an idea to a policy. The information was
already public. Flowery Branch puts agendas and minutes on CivicClerk, ordinances on
Municode, and permits and financial documents on the city website.

Following one issue from proposal to vote still meant moving between several systems and
reading a lot of dense documents. Those systems are built to keep an accurate record,
which is what they should be built for. This site does the other job. It puts the record
in one place and tries to make it easier to read.
